This release marks the **first public English edition** of *The Way of Investing: How to Think, Allocate Capital, and Survive Across Market Cycles*.

## About this release

Version 1.0 presents the book as a complete, integrated investment framework organized around three major areas:

- investment philosophy, behavioral finance, market cycles, and operating discipline;
- the TBSMV stock-selection framework; and
- portfolio architecture, risk management, and trading execution.

The English edition was independently reviewed, revised, and reorganized to improve clarity, reduce repetition, correct errors, strengthen references, and create a more coherent progression from investment thinking to capital allocation, risk control, and execution.
