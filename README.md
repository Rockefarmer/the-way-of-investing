## About the Book

*The Way of Investing* presents an integrated framework for investment thinking and practice. It connects investment philosophy, behavioral finance, business analysis, stock selection, portfolio construction, risk management, and trading execution.

The book is organized into three parts:

1. **Foundations** — investment philosophy, behavioral biases, market cycles, and operating discipline.
2. **Stock Selection System** — the TBSMV framework: Industry Trend, Business & Moat, Strategy, Management & Culture, and Valuation.
3. **Portfolio, Risk, and Execution** — portfolio architecture, risk management, position sizing, liquidity, drawdown control, and disciplined execution.

A central principle runs through the book:

> Long-term returns are possible only when the investor first learns how to survive.
